4. Endpoints

Primary: Progression-free survival (PFS) per RECIST v1.1 by blinded independent central review

Secondary:

  • Objective response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1
  • Overall survival (OS)
  • Incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events (CTCAE v5.0)

Candidate secondary (under consideration):

  • Patient-reported outcomes (EORTC QLQ-C30)
  • ctDNA dynamics as an early efficacy signal
  • PK exposure–response

5. Key eligibility criteria

  • (Inclusion) Histologically or cytologically confirmed metastatic NSCLC
  • (Inclusion) Progression on or after exactly one prior line of platinum-based therapy
  • (Inclusion) ECOG performance status 0–1
  • (Inclusion) Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1
  • (Inclusion) Adequate organ and marrow function
  • (Inclusion) Documented absence of active gastrointestinal disease that would impair oral therapy absorption — verification method NOT YET SPECIFIED — OPEN QUESTION: Medical wants objective GI verification. How do we confirm it without wrecking the enrollment timeline?
  • (Exclusion) Untreated central nervous system metastases
  • (Exclusion) Estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) below protocol floor

6. Schedule of assessments (sketch)

  • Screening (Day −28 to −1): consent, imaging, tissue/biomarker, labs, ECG
  • Treatment (21-day cycles): dosing, safety labs, AE review, PK (Arm A, C1–C2)
  • Tumor assessment: CT with contrast every 6 weeks through Week 48, then every 9 weeks
  • End of treatment + 30-day safety follow-up; survival follow-up every 9 weeks
